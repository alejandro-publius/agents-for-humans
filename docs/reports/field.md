## Does anything here change our framing

No. The two titles found are a cost-control agent that runs offline and a ledger agent with several
Strands agents and a pause that kept asking; the second overlaps with our human moment in one respect
(a pause the human must answer) and our package already treats "asked once" as a measured guarantee
(the interrupt keyed to a policy flag, the decision remembered per outage, the question withdrawn or
superseded when moot, 194 of 194 in the sweep). Nothing found is about transit, accessibility, or an
agency's published policy as the source of truth, and nothing found measures its guarantees. Our
framing stands: the person (a rider who depends on elevators), the guarantee (models propose, code
decides, measured), the number (from results files, checked by CI), the human moment (asked once, in
plain words). One line goes to `docs/reports/rubric-self-score.md` to say so.
