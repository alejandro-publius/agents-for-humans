# Picking the task

The brief: build an agent that **handles a repetitive task end to end in the background** and only
surfaces when a human decision is genuinely needed. Judges reward a credible, specific audience over
breadth. Pick one track, one persona, one task.

## Tracks (pick one on Devpost)

| Track | For | Organizer's examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Everyday Agents** | one person's home / money / health / errands / family | bills flagged or scheduled before they're late; household calendars kept in sync; returns handled before the window closes |
| **Professional Agents** | one professional's repetitive, judgment-heavy work | contractor compliance paperwork; teacher turning one lesson into 30 differentiated versions; solo researcher facing a mountain of papers; shop owner answering the same booking questions |
| **Good Neighbor Agents** | a group: nonprofit, food bank, school, library, local org | matching volunteers to shifts; answering member questions; turning scattered info into something anyone can ask |

## Decision template

- **Persona:** who exactly? (a named type of person, not "users")
- **The repetitive task:** what do they do over and over today, and how long does it take?
- **Inputs the agent needs:** where does the data come from (email, calendar, spreadsheet, API, PDFs)?
- **Autonomous actions:** what can it safely do without asking?
- **Handoff moments:** what decisions must stay with the human, and how are they surfaced?
- **Demo storyline (≤5 min):** problem → who → why it matters → agent running end to end.

## Scoring levers (from the rules)

- Non-trivial, genuine use of Strands (tools, sessions, handoff, maybe multi-agent).
- A live demo link and/or AgentCore deployment strengthens Technical Implementation.
- Complete product experience, not a proof of concept.
- Up to 0.6 bonus points for builder.aws.com posts titled with "Agents for Humans" (0.2 each).
